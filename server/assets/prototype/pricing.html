A little more clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Talk to the Impelo team ↗
What counts as a staff account?

Clinicians, reception staff and administrators count towards the staff allowance. Patient accounts do not. Clinic Network’s 40 staff accounts cover all included locations together.

What if we need more capacity?

Discuss additional locations, staff, usage and rollout support with us. Extra capacity, complex migration and custom integrations would be scoped separately.

What is included in the usage allowance?

Visit and document allowances are still to be confirmed. SMS and WhatsApp usage would be scoped separately. These example packages do not set an unlimited allowance.

Are mobile and desktop apps available?

Android, iOS, Windows and macOS apps are Coming Soon. See the app previews and planned platforms.

How does annual billing work?

The annual example reduces the monthly equivalent by 10%. The full yearly amount would be paid upfront. Select Annual above to see both the monthly equivalent and yearly total.

How We Shaped These Example Plans

Our proposed prices are an Impelo design hypothesis to test with practices, informed by published alternatives rather than a validated willingness-to-pay study. Their products cover different workflows, and vendor prices may change.

Local Entry Points

Welcome Desk lists Starter at R499/month for 3 users, Growth at R899 for 10 and Enterprise at R1,499 with unlimited seats. Its intake and booking tiers informed our capacity-based approach.

Practice-Scale Packaging

Baselayer.med lists Solo at R899/month for 2 users and Practice at R2,499 for unlimited users. Its broader clinical and accounting coverage is not a like-for-like Impelo comparison.

Separate Usage Costs

Zanda distinguishes Starter and Growth, practitioner capacity and paid SMS. The page reviewed displayed USD; we have not converted it into a South African quote.

Primary vendor pages reviewed 4 October 2026. Impelo prices, staff limits and the annual reduction are invented prototype assumptions. Validate supplier costs, buyer interviews, support workload and tax treatment before adopting them.

South African Sign Language is the twelfth official language. Text translation alone does not provide SASL access.

Prototype privacy

The prototype saves only language and display preferences in this browser. Demo form data stays in memory and is never sent or stored by this page. No live accounts, clinical records, payments, or messaging service are connected.
