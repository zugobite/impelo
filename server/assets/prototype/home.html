The Problem Behind The Platform.

Uncertainty affects people waiting for care and the teams coordinating it. Our exploratory surveys helped shape Impelo’s priorities.

Spent between one and four hours a day managing queues.

staff respondents

7 / 7

Received queue-status questions often or very often.

staff respondents

Small Sample, Clear Design Questions. These findings describe 10 patient/service-user respondents and 7 staff respondents in a convenience sample. They are not national statistics or evidence that Impelo has reduced waiting times.

Prototype privacy

The prototype saves only language and display preferences in this browser. Demo form data stays in memory and is never sent or stored by this page. No live accounts, clinical records, payments, or messaging service are connected.
