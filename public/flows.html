02 / PLATFORM SCREEN FLOWS

Every screen has a next step.

From first discovery to a completed visit: a connected blueprint for patients, clinic teams, and administrators.

Queue, booking, records, practices, patient lookup, and reports are planned. The previews below use illustrative content and do not carry out healthcare actions.

SCREEN INVENTORY

A complete screen inventory.

The URL labels distinguish established navigation from proposed deeper screens. Visual previews are deliberately schematic; exact fields require product and clinic validation.

Discover → choose a path Public visitor

  1. Public homepage

    /public-website

    Understand the problem, intended benefits, and product stage.

    Content: Who it serves · how it works · inclusion · product status

    Action: Explore the platform → How it works, About Impelo, Pricing or Contact

    Recovery: If the platform is not open for enrolment, offer an enquiry rather than a misleading sign-up button.

  2. How it works

    /how-it-works/

    See a simple patient journey and a separate clinic journey.

    Content: Visit timeline · queue explanation · records and permissions

    Action: Choose patient or practice information → Patient access information or practice enquiry

    Recovery: Do not imply that all clinics participate or that a queue position guarantees a time.

  3. About Impelo

    /about-impelo/

    Meet the six student builders and understand the research behind the project.

    Content: Names · student numbers · project roles · exploratory survey · source notes

    Action: Read the team and research context → Research limitations, platform direction or practice enquiry

    Recovery: Avatars are illustrative. Do not imply clinician endorsements or treat a small convenience sample as national data.

  4. Pricing & suitability

    /pricing/

    Let a practice understand what a plan could cover.

    Content: Confirmed plans · inclusions · exclusions · billing terms

    Action: Enquire about a practice plan → Contact form with selected topic

    Recovery: Until approved, display pricing to be confirmed. Do not show invented amounts.

  5. Contact

    /contact/

    Route a non-clinical enquiry to the right team.

    Content: Name · reply address · topic · message; organisation optional

    Action: Preview enquiry submission → Local demo receipt; no external message

    Recovery: The prototype validates example input, displays a demo receipt and clears the form. A production contact service and confirmed reply channel remain planned.

Account → Health Home Patient / invited staff

  1. Sign in

    /login

    Enter an existing account securely.

    Content: Email · password · visible labels · show password · recovery link

    Action: Sign in → Health Home or staff Dashboard based on authorised role

    Recovery: Explain sign-in failure in plain language without revealing whether another person has an account. Allow password managers and paste.

  2. Create account / invitation

    Proposed enrolment screen

    Establish a patient account or accept a staff invitation.

    Content: Minimum contact details · terms · preferred language; staff role assigned by invitation

    Action: Create account / accept invitation → Verification, then profile completion

    Recovery: Do not let a patient select a privileged staff role. Decide supported identity and contact alternatives before launch.

  3. Verify & complete profile

    Proposed verification / profile

    Confirm contact and gather only information needed for the service.

    Content: Verification status · preferred name · contact preferences · language

    Action: Continue → Health Home

    Recovery: Expired code: explain expiry and resend. No email access: offer a defined assisted recovery path, not a dead end.

  4. Health Home

    /dashboard

    Make the next useful action easy to find.

    Content: Upcoming visit · queue state · records shortcut · practice context

    Action: Open the relevant task → Appointments, Queue, Records, or Practices

    Recovery: New patient: show useful first steps. Loading or an error must not appear as no appointments.

  5. Profile & sign out

    /profile

    Review account details and end a session on a shared device.

    Content: Profile · contact preferences · display preferences · sign out

    Action: Save verified changes or sign out → Updated profile or sign-in screen

    Recovery: Explain which changes require verification. After sign-out, prevent the next user seeing protected data through back navigation.

Find care → plan a visit Patient

  1. Practices

    /practices

    Find a participating clinic and practical visit information.

    Content: Clinic name · services · location · access information · contact

    Action: View practice → Practice details

    Recovery: No nearby clinic: show a clear empty state and a useful contact route. Avoid promising availability not supplied by the clinic.

  2. Practice details

    Proposed practice detail

    Know where to go and what to expect.

    Content: Address · hours · directions in text · accessibility · booking rules

    Action: Choose visit type → Available appointments

    Recovery: Map or location permission unavailable: text directions still work. Show walk-in policies only when confirmed.

  3. Choose an appointment

    /appointments · proposed booking view

    Choose an available visit that fits the patient’s needs.

    Content: Visit type · available date/time · clinician where relevant

    Action: Review visit → Booking review

    Recovery: Slot taken: preserve selections and offer alternatives. Timezone and date labels must be explicit.

  4. Review & submit

    Proposed booking review

    Check the visit before sending.

    Content: Clinic · visit type · date/time · preparation · change policy

    Action: Request / confirm appointment → Confirmed booking after server acknowledgement

    Recovery: Connection lost: show not confirmed yet. Retry safely without creating duplicate appointments.

  5. Visit details / change

    /appointments · proposed detail

    Keep the booking, directions, and next steps together.

    Content: Confirmation reference · clinic · date/time · check-in instructions

    Action: Reschedule or cancel → Updated visit after confirmation

    Recovery: Show the effect before cancelling. If a change fails, the original confirmed booking remains visible.

Arrive → wait → be called Patient with clinic reception

  1. Arrival & check-in

    /queue · proposed check-in

    Connect an arrival to the correct clinic visit.

    Content: Clinic · visit reference or assisted identification · arrival confirmation

    Action: Check in → Ticket after reception/service confirmation

    Recovery: Already checked in: show the existing ticket. Wrong clinic or no booking: explain the assisted walk-in process.

  2. Queue ticket

    /queue

    Understand the current stage and the next action.

    Content: Ticket reference · waiting state · location · last updated · clinic instructions

    Action: View queue / ask reception for help → Waiting, called, or updated status

    Recovery: Do not show a patient’s clinical reason publicly. Use a non-identifying ticket on shared displays.

  3. Waiting

    /queue · proposed live state

    Make changes understandable without needing constant attention.

    Content: Current stage · last confirmed update · next action · optional estimate with uncertainty

    Action: Stay informed / refresh → Called state

    Recovery: Lost connection: retain last confirmed state with its timestamp and a stale-data warning. Queue order may change for clinical priority.

  4. Called / in consultation

    Proposed visit state

    Know where to go when the team is ready.

    Content: Room or reception instructions · visible called label

    Action: Follow clinic instructions → Consultation, then visit completed

    Recovery: Use visual text plus an agreed clinic announcement process. A missed call needs a staff resolution; no automatic punitive removal.

  5. Visit completed

    Proposed completion screen

    Know what happens after the visit.

    Content: Completion state · follow-up instructions when published · records link

    Action: View visit summary → Records or future appointment

    Recovery: Never imply the visit is complete because a timer elapsed. Staff must confirm the appropriate state.

Records → permission → continuity Patient / authorised care team

  1. Records list

    /records

    Find the patient’s available visit information.

    Content: Visit date · document type · publishing status · practice

    Action: Open a record → Record detail

    Recovery: No published documents: say so. Unavailable records and an empty list must be different states.

  2. Record detail

    Proposed record detail

    Read a published summary with clear context.

    Content: Title · date · source practice · readable content · supported download

    Action: Read or download deliberately → Detail / accessible document

    Recovery: Unreadable scan: provide a supported accessible alternative. Explain that a missing document may still be awaiting publication.

  3. Sharing choice

    /practices · proposed consent view

    Understand the scope of any optional record-sharing decision.

    Content: Recipient practice · information scope · purpose · duration where applicable

    Action: Review then grant or decline → Permission receipt

    Recovery: Use explicit labels and language the person understands. Optional sharing must not be bundled into unrelated account agreement.

  4. Review / withdraw permission

    Proposed sharing history

    See which optional permissions are active and request changes.

    Content: Active permissions · dates · scope · history

    Action: Withdraw where applicable → Updated permission after acknowledgement

    Recovery: Explain practical limits and applicable policy in reviewed wording. Do not promise deletion of records already lawfully retained by a clinic.

Prepare → coordinate → hand over Reception, nurse, practitioner

  1. Clinic Dashboard

    /dashboard

    Understand today’s work for the selected practice.

    Content: Practice context · schedule · waiting count · exceptions

    Action: Open schedule or queue → Appointments / Queue

    Recovery: No practice assigned: explain who can resolve it. Never mix another clinic’s patient data into the same view.

  2. Schedule & arrival

    /appointments

    Confirm planned visits and register arrivals.

    Content: Date · visit status · permitted patient identifiers

    Action: Confirm arrival → Queue entry

    Recovery: Potential duplicate identity: pause and follow the clinic’s matching procedure. Do not merge records casually.

  3. Queue board

    /queue

    Coordinate arrivals and patient stages.

    Content: Tickets · waiting/called states · assigned service · last update

    Action: Call / move stage with permission → Next operational state

    Recovery: Concurrent changes: refresh and explain conflict. Priority changes must follow clinic policy and authorised staff action.

  4. Patient lookup & record

    /patients

    Open only the information allowed for the current care context.

    Content: Search identifiers · identity confirmation · access status

    Action: Open permitted record → Clinical workspace / handoff

    Recovery: Access denied: explain the required process without leaking clinical information. Log access and meaningful changes.

  5. Close visit & handoff

    Proposed clinical completion

    Make the next team action and patient instructions clear.

    Content: Completion status · published summary · follow-up task owner

    Action: Complete authorised step → Updated Dashboard and patient view

    Recovery: Unsaved work needs an explicit warning and recovery design. Draft notes must not be presented as final patient instructions.

People → roles → practice oversight Authorised administrator

  1. Users list

    /users

    Review account entries in the existing users demo.

    Content: User list · selected account detail

    Action: Open account → User detail

    Recovery: Current demo is a foundation. Production filtering, invitations, role changes, and audit controls require verification.

  2. User detail & role

    /users/:id · proposed role controls

    Confirm the account and permitted responsibilities.

    Content: Identity · practice assignment · current role · change reason

    Action: Review and confirm an authorised change → Updated assignment

    Recovery: Prevent self-escalation and accidental removal of the last administrator. Server-side permission enforcement is required.

  3. Practice configuration

    /practices · proposed administration

    Define operational context and approved information.

    Content: Hours · services · staff assignment · public contact details

    Action: Review and save → Practice information after acknowledgement

    Recovery: Changes affecting visits need a clear effective date and ownership. Keep clinical and billing settings separate.

  4. Reports & audit

    /reports

    Review operational summaries using the minimum necessary information.

    Content: Date range · practice · report purpose · permission

    Action: View or export an authorised report → Report or confirmed download

    Recovery: Reports are planned. Avoid patient-identifying exports by default; show who can access, export, and retain each report.

Interrupted journey → safe recovery All signed-in users

  1. Connection interrupted

    Any action screen

    Distinguish unsent work from confirmed work.

    Content: Connection message · last confirmation time · draft or request status

    Action: Retry safely → Confirmed outcome or explicit failure

    Recovery: A spinning indicator is not proof of submission. Use request identifiers and duplicate prevention for bookings and check-in.

  2. Session ending

    Proposed session warning

    Allow a person time to keep working when policy permits.

    Content: Clear warning · remaining time · continue session action

    Action: Continue or sign out → Current screen or sign in

    Recovery: Explain how drafts are handled. Do not expose sensitive drafts in persistent browser storage on shared devices.

  3. Sign in again

    /login

    Resume only after identity is restored.

    Content: Sign-in fields · safe return destination

    Action: Sign in → Intended permitted screen

    Recovery: Recheck role and permission. Never put health details or secret tokens into the return URL.

  4. Help & assisted alternative

    Proposed consistent help entry

    Let a person complete the real-world task without resolving every digital obstacle alone.

    Content: Confirmed clinic contact · accessible help · assisted service details

    Action: Ask the appropriate team → Human support

    Recovery: Digital access failure must not automatically mean loss of care. Confirm actual clinic alternatives before publishing them.

NAVIGATION CONTRACT

Keep the platform familiar.

The current navigation is the starting point for this blueprint.

Patients

Health Home → Queue and Appointments → Records → Practices. Profile and sign-out remain in the account menu.

Clinic teams

Dashboard → Queue and Appointments → Patients → Practices → Reports and Users, subject to permissions.

Public visitors

Home → Platform / How it works → About the team → Pricing → Contact. The public site must offer a clear route to existing account sign-in.

On auth screens and in the sidebar, replace only the brand icon and preserve the existing structure and wording. Full logo lockups belong in documents and the identity reference.
